About

The Story behind the deck.

— Subrahamanyam ✦

I'm a presentation designer who genuinely believes a great slide is the difference between an idea being heard and an idea being remembered. For five years, I've been doing exactly that — shaping how founders pitch, how teams report, how brands speak on stage.

My path didn't start in a design school. I trained as a mechanical engineer — and somewhere between engineering drawings and 3D modelling coursework, I realized I was more excited by visual systems than mechanical ones. I taught myself design after graduating, started as a graphic designer, moved into motion, and landed at SlideSciences where I've grown from Presentation Specialist to Creative & Production Manager.

Today, I lead a multidisciplinary team, set design standards, and ship work for clients across pharma, SaaS, finance, and consulting. I care about narrative structure as much as visual craft — because the best-looking deck still fails if the story doesn't land.
